## 1.0 Purpose


Innovatech Solutions is committed to the health and well-being of our employees and their families. This policy provides comprehensive details about the company's healthcare benefits offerings, including medical, dental, vision, prescription drug coverage, Health Savings Accounts (HSA), Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA), and related programs. It outlines eligibility, enrollment procedures, plan options, costs, and employee rights.


This document supplements the general **Benefits & Insurance Policies (500)** and should be read in conjunction with it. Where a conflict exists, this healthcare-specific policy governs.

## 2.0 Eligibility


### 2.1 Employee Eligibility


Healthcare benefits are available to employees based on their employment classification and scheduled hours.


| Employment Type | Scheduled Hours | Benefits Eligibility | Waiting Period | Effective Date |

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| Full-Time | 30+ hours/week | Eligible for all medical, dental, and vision plans | 30 days | 1st of the month following 30 days of employment |

| Part-Time | 20–29 hours/week | Eligible for medical, dental, and vision plans (limited plan options) | 60 days | 1st of the month following 60 days of employment |

| Part-Time | Fewer than 20 hours/week | Not eligible | N/A | N/A |

| Temporary / Contract | Any | Not eligible | N/A | N/A |

| Intern | Any | Not eligible | N/A | N/A |


**Examples:**

- An employee hired on March 5 (full-time) becomes eligible on April 4 and benefits are effective May 1.

- An employee hired on March 5 (part-time, 25 hours/week) becomes eligible on May 4 and benefits are effective June 1.


### 2.2 Dependent Eligibility


Eligible dependents may be enrolled in medical, dental, and vision plans:


- **Legal spouse** (including same-sex spouses)

- **Domestic partner** (as defined by state law; an affidavit may be required)

- **Children** up to age 26 (biological, adopted, stepchildren, or legal wards)

- **Children of any age** who are permanently and totally disabled, if the disability occurred before age 26

- **Disabled dependents** must be verified annually through the insurance carrier


**Documentation:** Proof of dependent eligibility (marriage certificate, birth certificate, domestic partner affidavit, or court order) must be submitted within 30 days of the qualifying life event or during the initial enrollment period.


### 2.3 Special Eligibility Situations


- **COBRA Continuation:** Employees who lose coverage due to termination (other than gross misconduct), reduction in hours, or other qualifying events may continue coverage under COBRA for up to 18 months (or 36 months for dependents in certain cases). See Section 10.0.

- **Leave of Absence (FMLA, medical, personal):** Benefits may continue during approved leaves. Contact P&C for details on premium payment arrangements.

- **Rehired Employees:** Employees rehired within 30 days of termination may have their prior benefits reinstated without a new waiting period, subject to carrier approval.

### 3.2 Plan Descriptions


#### 3.2.1 PPO Gold

- **Best for:** Employees who want predictable copays, low deductibles, and the flexibility to see any provider without a referral.

- **Network:** National PPO network with access to over 1 million providers.

- **Out-of-Network:** Covered at 60% after deductible (higher out-of-pocket costs apply).

- **Ideal for:** Families with frequent medical needs or those who travel frequently.


#### 3.2.2 PPO Silver

- **Best for:** Employees who want moderate premiums with moderate out-of-pocket costs.

- **Network:** Same national PPO network as Gold.

- **Out-of-Network:** Covered at 50% after deductible.

- **Ideal for:** Employees who are generally healthy but want flexibility and a lower premium.


#### 3.2.3 HMO Standard

- **Best for:** Employees who want the lowest premiums and are comfortable using a defined network of providers.

- **Network:** Regional HMO network (available in CA, NY, IL, TX, FL, WA, and CO).

- **Referral Required:** Yes, for all specialist visits.

- **Out-of-Network:** Not covered (except emergencies).

- **Ideal for:** Employees who live in a service area and want predictable, low-cost care.


#### 3.2.4 HDHP (High Deductible Health Plan)

- **Best for:** Employees who want the lowest premiums, are generally healthy, and want to save for future medical expenses through an HSA.

- **Network:** National PPO network.

- **Out-of-Network:** Covered at 60% after deductible.

- **HSA:** Eligible for a Health Savings Account with a company contribution of $500 annually.

- **Ideal for:** Employees who want to maximize tax-advantaged savings and are comfortable with higher deductibles.

## 7.0 Health Savings Account (HSA)


### 7.1 Eligibility

Only employees enrolled in the **HDHP** are eligible for an HSA. You cannot be enrolled in an HSA if you are:

- Covered by another non-HDHP medical plan

- Enrolled in Medicare

- Claimed as a dependent on someone else's tax return

- Enrolled in a general-purpose Healthcare FSA (limited-purpose FSA is allowed)


### 7.2 Company Contribution

Innovatech Solutions contributes **$500 annually** to your HSA. Contributions are made in equal installments throughout the year.


### 7.3 Contribution Limits (2024)

- **Individual:** $4,150

- **Family:** $8,300

- **Catch-up (age 55+):** Additional $1,000


### 7.4 HSA Advantages

- Tax-deductible contributions

- Tax-free growth

- Tax-free withdrawals for qualified medical expenses

- Funds roll over year to year

- Portable (you own the account, even if you leave Innovatech)


### 7.5 HSA Debit Card

A debit card is provided for easy access to HSA funds. Receipts should be retained for tax purposes.

## 8.0 Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)


### 8.1 Healthcare FSA

- **Eligibility:** All employees enrolled in a non-HDHP medical plan.

- **Contribution Limit (2024):** $3,200

- **Use:** Reimbursement for qualified medical, dental, and vision expenses.

- **Rollover:** Up to $640 may roll over to the next plan year.

- **Grace Period:** None (use-it-or-lose-it applies to remaining funds).


### 8.2 Dependent Care FSA

- **Eligibility:** All employees.

- **Contribution Limit (2024):** $5,000 per household (or $2,500 if married filing separately).

- **Use:** Reimbursement for daycare, after-school care, summer day camp, and elder care expenses.

- **Rollover:** Not permitted.

- **Grace Period:** None.


### 8.3 Limited-Purpose FSA

- **Eligibility:** Employees enrolled in the HDHP who want to save for dental and vision expenses.

- **Contribution Limit (2024):** $3,200

- **Use:** Dental and vision expenses only (not medical).

- **Rollover:** Up to $640 may roll over.

## 10.0 COBRA Continuation Coverage


### 10.1 Eligibility

COBRA continuation coverage is available to employees and dependents who lose coverage due to:

- Termination of employment (other than gross misconduct)

- Reduction in hours

- Death of the covered employee

- Divorce or legal separation

- Dependent child reaching age 26


### 10.2 Duration

- **Employees:** Up to 18 months

- **Dependents:** Up to 36 months in certain cases (e.g., divorce, death, aging out)


### 10.3 Cost

COBRA participants pay the full premium (employee + employer share) plus a 2% administrative fee.


### 10.4 Notification

The P&C team will notify eligible individuals within 14 days of the qualifying event. Employees must elect COBRA within 60 days of the notice.

## 11.0 Enrollment Procedures


### 11.1 New Hire Enrollment

- **Deadline:** Within 30 days of your benefits eligibility date.

- **How to Enroll:** Log in to **WorkDay** and navigate to the Benefits section.

- **Required Documentation:** Dependent verification documents must be uploaded during enrollment.

- **Failure to Enroll:** If you do not enroll within 30 days, you will be defaulted into the HMO Standard plan (if available in your area) or the PPO Silver plan.


### 11.2 Open Enrollment

- **Period:** Annually, November 1–15.

- **Effective Date:** January 1 of the following year.

- **Changes Allowed:** You may change plans, add or remove dependents, and adjust HSA/FSA contributions.

- **Default:** If you do not make changes, you will remain in your current plan (except FSA, which requires annual re-election).


### 11.3 Qualifying Life Events (QLE)

You may make changes to your benefits outside of Open Enrollment if you experience a qualifying life event within 30 days of the event.


| Qualifying Life Event | Examples |

| :--- | :--- |

| **Marriage** | Adding a spouse to your plan |

| **Divorce** | Removing a spouse from your plan |

| **Birth or Adoption** | Adding a child to your plan |

| **Death of a Dependent** | Removing a dependent from your plan |

| **Loss of Other Coverage** | Losing coverage through a spouse's plan |

| **Change in Employment Status** | Moving from part-time to full-time |

| **Change in Residence** | Moving to a new state or HMO service area |

| **Medicare Eligibility** | Enrolling in Medicare |


**Procedure:** Submit a QLE request in WorkDay within 30 days of the event. Supporting documentation is required.

## 13.0 Coordination of Benefits


If you or your dependents are covered under another health plan (e.g., through a spouse's employer or Medicare), the two plans coordinate to determine which pays first.


- **Primary Plan:** The plan covering the employee as the primary subscriber pays first.

- **Secondary Plan:** The other plan pays second, covering remaining eligible expenses.

- **Medicare:** For active employees age 65+, Innovatech's plan is primary, and Medicare is secondary.


You must report other coverage to the P&C team within 30 days of enrollment.

## 14.0 Appeals and Grievances


### 14.1 Internal Appeals

If a claim is denied, you may file an internal appeal with the insurance carrier within **180 days** of the denial.


- **Step 1:** Contact the carrier directly to request a review.

- **Step 2:** If the denial is upheld, you may request a second-level appeal.

- **Step 3:** If the denial is still upheld, you may request an external review.


### 14.2 External Review

External reviews are conducted by an independent organization. You may request an external review if:

- The internal appeal process is exhausted

- The denial involves a medical necessity determination

- The denial involves an experimental or investigational treatment


### 14.3 Assistance

The P&C team can assist you with the appeals process. Contact `hr@innovatech.example.com` for support.

## 17.0 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


**Q: When do my benefits start?**

A: Full-time employees: 1st of the month following 30 days of employment. Part-time (20–29 hours): 1st of the month following 60 days of employment.


**Q: Can I add my domestic partner to my plan?**

A: Yes, if your domestic partnership is legally recognized in your state. An affidavit may be required.


**Q: What happens if I miss the 30-day enrollment window?**

A: You will be defaulted into the HMO Standard plan (if available) or the PPO Silver plan. You will not be able to change plans until Open Enrollment or a qualifying life event.


**Q: Can I change plans mid-year?**

A: Only if you experience a qualifying life event (e.g., marriage, birth, loss of other coverage).


**Q: How do I find an in-network provider?**

A: Visit the WellnessCorp provider directory or contact the carrier directly.


**Q: Is my spouse eligible for coverage if they have access to their own employer's plan?**

A: Yes, your spouse can be covered under our plan even if they have access to their own employer's plan. However, they may not be eligible for the employer premium contribution if they have access to affordable coverage elsewhere (spousal surcharge may apply).


**Q: What is the difference between an HSA and an FSA?**

A: An HSA is tied to the HDHP, rolls over year to year, and is portable. An FSA is a use-it-or-lose-it account with a small rollover allowance. You cannot have both a general-purpose FSA and an HSA.


**Q: How do I submit a claim?**

A: Most in-network claims are filed by the provider. For out-of-network claims, submit a claim form to the carrier within 90 days of the service date.


**Q: What if I have a question about a denied claim?**

A: Contact the carrier first. If you need assistance, contact the P&C team at `hr@innovatech.example.com`.
