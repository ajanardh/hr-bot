# 400 - Expenses & Reimbursement Policy


## 1.0 Purpose

This policy governs the reimbursement of expenses incurred by employees for approved business purposes. Innovatech expects employees to exercise good judgment and spend company funds responsibly.


## 2.0 Scope

This policy applies to all employees. All expenses must be pre-approved by a manager and comply with the guidelines below.


## 3.0 General Guidelines

- **Documentation:** An itemized receipt is required for all expenses over $25. Credit card statements are not sufficient.

- **Submission:** All expense reports must be submitted through **Concur** within **30 days** of the expense date.

- **Approval:** Your direct manager is responsible for approving your expense reports.

- **Timeliness:** Expenses submitted more than 60 days after being incurred may not be reimbursed.


## 4.0 Specific Expense Categories


### 4.1 Home Office Equipment

For approved remote or hybrid employees, Innovatech offers a one-time stipend of **$500** to set up a home office.


- **Eligible Items:** Monitor, keyboard, mouse, webcam, headset, desk lamp, ergonomic chair.

- **Ineligible Items:** Desks, internet service, coffee makers, general office supplies.

- **Procedure:** Purchase the items personally, then submit a single expense report in Concur with the required receipts. The expense category should be "Home Office Stipend."


### 4.2 Travel Expenses

All travel must be pre-approved. Refer to the **Travel & Entertainment Policy (900)** for detailed rules on booking flights, hotels, and ground transportation. Generally:

- Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours.

- Hotel rates should not exceed $300 per night (or the GSA rate for the location, whichever is higher).

- Meals are reimbursed on a per diem basis of $75 per day.


### 4.3 Professional Development

- **Courses & Certifications:** Reimbursable with prior manager approval.

- **Conferences:** Travel and registration fees are reimbursable with prior manager approval.


### 4.4 Client Entertainment

These expenses are highly scrutinized. They must have a clear business purpose, be reasonable, and be pre-approved by a manager. The expense report must list the names and companies of all attendees.


## 5.0 Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following items are **not** reimbursable:

- Personal entertainment (e.g., in-flight movies, gym memberships)

- Traffic and parking fines

- Personal clothing (even if required for work, unless it is a uniform)

- Lost personal items

- Expenses for a spouse or partner
