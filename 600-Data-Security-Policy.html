# 600 - Data Security & Acceptable Use Policy


## 1.0 Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to protect Innovatech Solutions' information assets from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction. This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who access company data or systems.


## 2.0 Scope

This policy covers all company-owned or leased devices, networks, and data, as well as personal devices used for company business (BYOD).


## 3.0 Core Security Requirements


### 3.1 Acceptable Use

- Company devices and accounts are for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted, provided it does not interfere with work, consume excessive resources, or violate any other policy.

- Employees must not use company systems to engage in illegal activities, access inappropriate content, or send harassing or discriminatory material.


### 3.2 Device Security

- All company-issued laptops and mobile devices must have full-disk encryption, a screen lock, and up-to-date antivirus software enabled. IT enforces these settings via Mobile Device Management (MDM).

- Personal devices used for work (BYOD) must be enrolled in our MDM system to ensure they meet security standards.

- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Helpdesk and Security Team immediately.


### 3.3 Network Security

- **VPN:** Employees working remotely must connect to the company network via the **GlobalProtect VPN** for all work-related activities. Public Wi-Fi networks should be avoided; if used, a VPN is mandatory.

- **Wi-Fi:** The office Wi-Fi is secured. Do not share the guest Wi-Fi password with unauthorized individuals.


### 3.4 Data Handling and Classification

Data is classified into three levels. All employees must handle data according to its classification.

- **Public:** Information approved for public release (e.g., marketing materials).

- **Internal:** Information for internal use only (e.g., org charts, project plans, internal emails).

- **Confidential:** Highly sensitive information (e.g., PII, financial data, source code, strategic plans, client data). This data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.


### 3.5 Password Management

- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.

- Do not reuse passwords across personal and professional accounts.

- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts.

- Use the company-provided password manager (**1Password**) to store and share credentials.


## 4.0 Incident Reporting

Any suspected or actual security incident (e.g., phishing email, malware, lost device, data breach) must be reported to the **Security Team** (`security@innovatech.example.com`) within **1 hour** of discovery.


## 5.0 Enforcement

Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment and legal action.
