# 700 - Onboarding & Offboarding Procedures


## 1.0 Onboarding


### 1.1 Purpose

To ensure new employees are integrated into Innovatech Solutions smoothly and effectively, providing them with the tools, information, and connections they need to succeed.


### 1.2 Pre-Arrival (Before Day 1)

- **P&C:** Sends welcome email with first-day instructions, completes background check, and ships company laptop and welcome kit.

- **Manager:** Prepares team for new hire's arrival, assigns an "onboarding buddy," and schedules first-week meetings.

- **IT:** Creates user accounts (email, Slack, WorkDay) and prepares equipment.


### 1.3 Day 1 Activities

- Welcome meeting with P&C.

- IT setup and security training.

- Team introductions and lunch with onboarding buddy.

- Review of first-week goals with manager.


### 1.4 First Week & Month

- Complete mandatory compliance training (Code of Conduct, Data Security).

- Meet with key stakeholders.

- Begin working on initial projects.


## 2.0 Offboarding


### 2.1 Purpose

To ensure a smooth and professional transition for departing employees and to protect company assets and information.


### 2.2 Resignation

Employees are requested to provide a minimum of **4 weeks' written notice** to their manager and the P&C team. This allows for knowledge transfer and a smooth transition.


### 2.3 Offboarding Process

- **Manager:** Accepts resignation, plans knowledge transfer, and collects any direct reports' feedback.

- **P&C:** Conducts an exit interview, processes final pay (including PTO payout), and provides information on benefits continuation (COBRA).

- **IT:** Schedules deactivation of accounts and remote wipe of company devices.

- **Employee:** Returns all company property, including laptop, monitor, access badge, and corporate credit card.


### 2.4 Final Paycheck

The final paycheck, including accrued but unused PTO, will be issued in accordance with state law and company pay periods.
