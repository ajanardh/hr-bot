## 1.0 Purpose


Innovatech Solutions is committed to helping employees plan for a secure financial future. The Innovatech Solutions 401(k) Retirement Savings Plan (the "Plan") is a tax-advantaged retirement savings program designed to encourage long-term savings through employee contributions, company matching contributions, and a range of investment options.


This policy outlines eligibility, contribution rules, company match, vesting, investment options, loans, withdrawals, and other important Plan details. It supplements the general **Benefits & Insurance Policies (500)** and should be read in conjunction with it. Where a conflict exists, the official Plan Document governs.

## 3.0 Eligibility


### 3.1 Employee Eligibility


| Employment Type | Eligibility |

| :--- | :--- |

| Full-Time (30+ hours/week) | Eligible after 30 days of employment |

| Part-Time (20–29 hours/week) | Eligible after 30 days of employment |

| Part-Time (fewer than 20 hours/week) | Eligible after 1 year of service (1,000 hours) |

| Temporary / Contract | Not eligible |

| Intern | Not eligible |

| Leased Employees | Not eligible |


### 3.2 Entry Dates

Eligible employees may enroll on the **1st of the month** following their eligibility date.


**Example:** An employee hired on March 5 becomes eligible on April 4 and may enroll effective May 1.


### 3.3 Automatic Enrollment

Newly eligible employees are **automatically enrolled** at a **3% pre-tax deferral rate** unless they opt out or elect a different rate within 30 days of their eligibility date. Automatic enrollment is designed to encourage retirement savings. Employees may change or stop their contributions at any time.


### 3.4 Automatic Escalation

To help employees save more over time, the Plan includes an **automatic escalation feature**:


- Starting on January 1 following the first full year of enrollment, the deferral rate increases by **1% annually**.

- Escalation continues until the deferral rate reaches **10%**.

- Employees may opt out of automatic escalation at any time in their Fidelity account.


### 3.5 Rehired Employees

Employees rehired within 12 months of termination retain their prior service credit and vesting status. Rehired employees must re-enroll if they wish to resume contributions.

## 4.0 Employee Contributions


### 4.1 Contribution Types


| Type | Description | Tax Treatment |

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Pre-Tax (Traditional)** | Contributions made with pre-tax dollars | Reduces current taxable income; taxed at withdrawal |

| **Roth (After-Tax)** | Contributions made with after-tax dollars | No current tax benefit; tax-free withdrawals in retirement (if qualified) |

| **After-Tax (Non-Roth)** | Additional after-tax contributions (if plan permits) | Contributions grow tax-deferred; taxed at withdrawal |


Employees may split contributions between Pre-Tax and Roth accounts in any proportion, up to the annual IRS limit.


### 4.2 Contribution Limits (2024)


| Contribution Type | Limit |

| :--- | :--- |

| **Employee Elective Deferral (Pre-Tax + Roth combined)** | $23,000 |

| **Catch-Up Contribution (age 50+)** | Additional $7,500 |

| **Total Annual Additions (Employee + Employer)** | $69,000 (or 100% of compensation, whichever is less) |

| **Total with Catch-Up (age 50+)** | $76,500 |

| **Highly Compensated Employee (HCE) Limit** | May be subject to additional limits based on IRS testing |


**Note:** Contribution limits are indexed annually by the IRS and are subject to change.


### 4.3 Compensation Definition

"Eligible compensation" for contribution and match purposes includes:


- Base salary

- Overtime pay

- Bonuses and commissions

- Paid Time Off (PTO) pay


**Excluded from eligible compensation:**

- Severance pay

- Reimbursements (travel, expense)

- Fringe benefits

- Signing bonuses (unless specified)


### 4.4 Changing Contributions

Employees may change their contribution rate or type at any time by logging into their Fidelity account or contacting Fidelity directly. Changes take effect on the next available payroll cycle.

## 5.0 Company Matching Contributions


### 5.1 Match Formula

Innovatech Solutions matches **100% of the first 4% of eligible compensation** that an employee contributes to the Plan.


**Examples:**


| Employee Contribution Rate | Company Match Rate | Total Contribution |

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| 0% | 0% | 0% |

| 2% | 2% | 4% |

| 4% | 4% | 8% |

| 6% | 4% (capped at 4%) | 10% |

| 10% | 4% (capped at 4%) | 14% |


### 5.2 Match Frequency

The company match is calculated and deposited on a **per-pay-period basis**.


### 5.3 True-Up Match

Innovatech offers a **true-up match** at the end of the Plan Year (typically by March 15 of the following year). If you front-loaded your contributions early in the year and did not receive the full match due to per-pay-period caps, the true-up ensures you receive the full match you are entitled to, provided you were employed on the last day of the Plan Year.


### 5.4 Vesting of Company Match

Company matching contributions are **100% vested immediately**. Employees retain the full match even if they leave the company.


### 5.5 Match Eligibility

To receive the match in a given pay period, you must:


- Be an active employee on the payroll date

- Have an active deferral election in place

- Meet the contribution threshold (at least 4% of eligible compensation)

## 8.0 Loans


### 8.1 Loan Eligibility

Active employees with a vested account balance of at least **$2,000** may take a loan from their 401(k) account.


### 8.2 Loan Amount

- **Minimum loan:** $1,000

- **Maximum loan:** 50% of your vested account balance, up to a maximum of $50,000

- **Outstanding loans:** If you have an existing loan, the maximum is reduced by the highest outstanding loan balance in the prior 12 months


### 8.3 Loan Types


| Loan Type | Repayment Term | Interest Rate |

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **General Purpose Loan** | 1–5 years | Prime + 1% |

| **Primary Residence Loan** | 6–15 years | Prime + 1% |


### 8.4 Loan Repayment

- Loans are repaid through **after-tax payroll deductions**.

- Repayment begins on the first payroll after the loan is issued.

- If you leave Innovatech, the outstanding loan balance must be repaid within **60 days** or it will be treated as a taxable distribution (and may be subject to a 10% early withdrawal penalty if you are under age 59½).


### 8.5 Loan Fees

- **Loan origination fee:** $50

- **Annual maintenance fee:** $25

- **Loan default fee:** $100


### 8.6 Number of Loans

Employees may have a maximum of **two outstanding loans** at any time.

## 9.0 Withdrawals


### 9.1 In-Service Withdrawals

Active employees may withdraw from certain contribution sources while still employed:


| Source | In-Service Withdrawal Allowed? |

| :--- | :--- |

| Employee Pre-Tax Contributions | No (except hardship) |

| Employee Roth Contributions | No (except hardship) |

| Employee After-Tax Contributions | Yes (if plan permits) |

| Rollover Contributions | Yes |

| Company Match | No (except hardship) |


### 9.2 Hardship Withdrawals

Hardship withdrawals are permitted for **immediate and heavy financial needs** as defined by the IRS. Qualifying events include:


- Medical expenses for you, your spouse, or dependents

- Costs related to the purchase of a primary residence

- Tuition and related educational fees

- Preventative foreclosure or eviction from primary residence

- Funeral expenses

- Repair of damage to primary residence

- Certain expenses related to a federally declared disaster


**Hardship withdrawal rules:**

- You must first exhaust all other available resources (including loans).

- The withdrawal is limited to the amount necessary to satisfy the need.

- Hardship withdrawals are **taxable** and may be subject to a **10% early withdrawal penalty** if you are under age 59½.

- **Suspension:** After a hardship withdrawal, your contributions are suspended for **6 months**.


### 9.3 Age 59½ Withdrawals

If you are age 59½ or older and still employed, you may take an in-service withdrawal from your vested account balance. These withdrawals are taxable but not subject to the 10% early withdrawal penalty.


### 9.4 Termination Withdrawals

Upon termination of employment, you may:


- Leave your funds in the Plan (if balance is $7,000 or more)

- Roll over your balance to an IRA or another employer's plan

- Take a cash distribution (subject to taxes and possible penalties)


**Small Balance Force-Out:** If your vested balance is **$7,000 or less** and you do not elect a distribution, the Plan may automatically roll your balance into an IRA or distribute it in cash (if under $1,000).

## 10.0 Rollovers


### 10.1 Rollovers Into the Plan

New employees may roll over eligible assets from:


- Prior employer 401(k) plans

- 403(b) plans

- Governmental 457(b) plans

- Traditional IRAs

- Thrift Savings Plan (TSP)


**Procedure:** Contact Fidelity to initiate a rollover. You will need to provide a statement from your prior plan.


### 10.2 Rollovers Out of the Plan

If you leave Innovatech, you may roll your 401(k) balance into:


- A traditional IRA

- A Roth IRA (for Roth contributions)

- A new employer's 401(k) plan


Rollovers are generally tax-free if done correctly (direct rollover).

## 11.0 Beneficiary Designations


### 11.1 Importance of Beneficiary Designation

Your beneficiary designation determines who receives your 401(k) balance in the event of your death. This is separate from your will and must be kept up to date.


### 11.2 Default Beneficiary

If you do not designate a beneficiary, the Plan's default order is:


1. Spouse (if married)

2. Children (equally)

3. Parents (equally)

4. Estate


### 11.3 Changing Your Beneficiary

You may change your beneficiary at any time by logging into your Fidelity account. Beneficiary changes take effect immediately upon receipt.


### 11.4 Spousal Consent

If you are married and wish to designate someone other than your spouse as your primary beneficiary, your spouse must provide **notarized written consent**.

## 13.0 Compliance and Legal


### 13.1 ERISA

The Plan is governed by the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 (ERISA). As a Plan participant, you have certain rights under ERISA, including the right to:


- Receive a copy of the Plan Document

- Receive a summary of the Plan's annual financial report

- File a claim for benefits

- Appeal a denied claim


### 13.2 Nondiscrimination Testing

The Plan is subject to annual IRS nondiscrimination testing to ensure it does not disproportionately favor highly compensated employees (HCEs). If testing results require corrections, HCE contributions may be limited or refunded.


### 13.3 Fiduciary Responsibility

The Plan's fiduciaries are required to act in the best interest of Plan participants. Innovatech's Retirement Plan Committee oversees the Plan's investment menu and administration.


### 13.4 QDROs

Qualified Domestic Relations Orders (QDROs) may require a portion of your 401(k) balance to be paid to a former spouse. Contact Fidelity for QDRO procedures.

## 14.0 Leaving the Company


### 14.1 Termination

Upon termination of employment, you have the following options for your 401(k) balance:


| Option | Description |

| :--- | :--- |

| **Leave funds in the Plan** | Available if balance is $7,000 or more |

| **Roll over to an IRA** | Direct rollover — no taxes or penalties |

| **Roll over to new employer's plan** | If the new plan accepts rollovers |

| **Cash distribution** | Subject to income tax and possible 10% penalty |


### 14.2 Vesting on Termination

All employee contributions and company matching contributions are **100% vested** immediately, so you retain the full balance upon termination.


### 14.3 Final Contributions

Your final paycheck may include a 401(k) deduction for the final pay period. Company match on that contribution will be deposited according to the normal payroll schedule.

## 15.0 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


**Q: When am I eligible to join the 401(k) plan?**

A: Full-time and part-time employees (20+ hours/week) are eligible after 30 days of employment. Part-time employees working fewer than 20 hours/week are eligible after 1 year of service (1,000 hours).


**Q: What is the company match?**

A: Innovatech matches 100% of the first 4% of eligible compensation you contribute. The match is 100% vested immediately.


**Q: Can I contribute to both a Traditional and Roth 401(k)?**

A: Yes. You may split your contributions between Pre-Tax and Roth in any proportion, up to the annual IRS limit.


**Q: What is the 2024 contribution limit?**

A: $23,000 for employees under 50, plus an additional $7,500 catch-up contribution for employees age 50 and older.


**Q: Is there an automatic enrollment feature?**

A: Yes. Newly eligible employees are automatically enrolled at 3% pre-tax unless they opt out or elect a different rate.


**Q: What is automatic escalation?**

A: Your contribution rate automatically increases by 1% per year (up to 10%) unless you opt out.


**Q: How do I change my contribution rate?**

A: Log into your Fidelity account or contact Fidelity directly. Changes take effect on the next payroll cycle.


**Q: Can I take a loan from my 401(k)?**

A: Yes, if you have a vested balance of at least $2,000. You may borrow up to 50% of your vested balance, up to $50,000. Loans are repaid through payroll deductions.


**Q: Can I take a hardship withdrawal?**

A: Yes, for qualifying events such as medical expenses, tuition, or preventing foreclosure. Hardship withdrawals are taxable and may be subject to a 10% penalty if you are under 59½.


**Q: What happens to my 401(k) if I leave Innovatech?**

A: You may leave your funds in the Plan (if balance is $7,000+), roll them into an IRA or new employer's plan, or take a cash distribution. All contributions are 100% vested.


**Q: How do I designate a beneficiary?**

A: Log into your Fidelity account and update your beneficiary designation. If you are married and wish to name someone other than your spouse, notarized spousal consent is required.


**Q: Is there a true-up match?**

A: Yes. If you front-load your contributions and miss out on the full match due to per-pay-period caps, a true-up match is applied at year-end, provided you are employed on the last day of the Plan Year.


**Q: What is the default investment if I don't choose?**

A: Contributions are automatically invested in the Fidelity Freedom Fund closest to your projected retirement year.


**Q: Can I roll over my old 401(k) into this plan?**

A: Yes. Contact Fidelity to initiate a rollover from a prior employer's plan or an IRA.


**Q: How do I get help with my 401(k)?**

A: Contact Fidelity at 1-800-555-0600 or visit `fidelity.com/innovatech`. You may also contact the P&C team at `hr@innovatech.example.com` for general questions.
