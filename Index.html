# Innovatech Solutions - Employee Policy & Procedure Manual


**Version:** 4.1

**Last Updated:** October 26, 2023

**Effective Date:** November 1, 2023


## Introduction


Welcome to Innovatech Solutions. This manual outlines the policies, procedures, and benefits that govern your employment with us. Our goal is to provide a clear, consistent, and equitable framework that supports our employees and aligns with our company values.


This document is the master index. All employees are expected to be familiar with the policies contained herein. If you have questions, please consult this manual first, then reach out to your manager or the People & Culture (P&C) team.
