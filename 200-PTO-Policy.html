# 200 - Time Off & Leave Policies (PTO)


## 1.0 Purpose

Innovatech Solutions recognizes the importance of work-life balance and provides a comprehensive Paid Time Off (PTO) program to allow employees to rest, recharge, and attend to personal matters.


## 2.0 Scope

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time regular employees. Temporary employees and contractors are not eligible for PTO unless specified in their contract.


## 3.0 Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual


### 3.1 Accrual Rates

PTO is accrued based on the employee's length of service.


| Years of Service | Annual PTO Accrual | Accrual per Pay Period (Semi-Monthly) |

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| 0 - 2 years | 15 days (120 hours) | 5 hours |

| 3 - 5 years | 20 days (160 hours) | 6.67 hours |

| 6+ years | 25 days (200 hours) | 8.33 hours |


### 3.2 PTO Usage

- PTO can be used for any reason, including vacation, personal time, and short-term illness.

- Employees should request PTO as far in advance as possible.

- **Approval:** All PTO requests are subject to manager approval based on business needs.

  - For requests of 1-3 days: A minimum of 5 business days' notice is required.

  - For requests of 4+ days: A minimum of 10 business days' notice is required.

- Unused PTO can be carried over to the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 1.5 times the annual accrual rate (e.g., 180 hours for a 0-2 year employee).


### 3.3 PTO Request Procedure

1.  Submit a formal request through the **WorkDay** portal.

2.  Your manager will receive an automated notification to review the request.

3.  You will be notified via WorkDay once your request is approved or denied.


## 4.0 Company Holidays

Innovatech Solutions observes the following paid holidays each year. The office is closed on these days.


- New Year's Day

- Martin Luther King Jr. Day

- Presidents' Day

- Memorial Day

- Juneteenth

- Independence Day

- Labor Day

- Thanksgiving Day

- Day after Thanksgiving

- Christmas Eve

- Christmas Day


**Holiday Schedule:** If a holiday falls on a Saturday, it is typically observed on the preceding Friday. If it falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday. The official holiday schedule is published by the P&C team each December for the upcoming year.


## 5.0 Other Leave Types


### 5.1 Sick Leave

Sick leave is not separate from PTO. Employees use their accrued PTO for sick days. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days due to illness, a doctor's note may be required.


### 5.2 Parental Leave

Eligible employees who have been with the company for at least 12 months are entitled to 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or placement of a child. This is in addition to any PTO. Contact P&C for details.


### 5.3 Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling). Up to 3 days are available for extended family members.


### 5.4 Jury Duty

Employees will be granted paid time off to serve on a jury. You must provide a copy of the summons to your manager and P&C.


### 5.5 Leave of Absence (LOA)

For extended personal leaves or medical leaves (e.g., FMLA), employees must contact the P&C team to discuss options and eligibility.


### 5.6 Unpaid Time Off

If a time-off request exceeds the employee's available PTO balance, the employee may ask to take the uncovered hours as unpaid time off.

- Use available PTO first. The hours beyond that balance can be requested as unpaid time off.

- Unpaid time off uses the same notice as PTO: 5 business days for 1-3 days, and 10 business days for 4 or more days. The manager approves or denies the request in WorkDay.

- Unpaid hours are not paid and do not count as accrued PTO.

- If the absence is longer than 4 weeks, or it is for a medical or family reason, contact People & Culture. That request is handled as a Leave of Absence under section 5.5, not as ordinary unpaid time off.


## 6.0 Payout of PTO

Upon termination of employment, employees will be paid for accrued, unused PTO up to the maximum carryover limit, in accordance with state law.
