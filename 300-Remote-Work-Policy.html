# 300 - Remote & Hybrid Work Policy


## 1.0 Purpose

Innovatech Solutions supports a flexible work environment. This policy defines the guidelines and eligibility for remote and hybrid work arrangements to ensure productivity, collaboration, and compliance.


## 2.0 Scope

This policy applies to all employees whose roles are designated as "remote-eligible" by their department head.


## 3.0 Eligibility

Remote work is a privilege, not a right. Eligibility is determined by the nature of the role, employee performance, and business needs. Your manager and department head must approve any remote work arrangement.


- **Hybrid:** Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.

- **Fully Remote:** Employees may be approved to work remotely 100% of the time. This is typically reserved for roles that do not require a physical presence.


## 4.0 General Expectations for Remote Work

- **Availability:** Remote employees must be available during their standard working hours and be responsive via email, chat, and video calls.

- **Workspace:** Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe, ergonomic, and productive home workspace. Refer to the **IT & Equipment Policy (800)** for home office setup allowances.

- **Security:** All work must be conducted on company-approved devices and networks. Refer to the **Data Security Policy (600)** for specific requirements.

- **Performance:** All performance standards and expectations remain the same, regardless of work location.


## 5.0 Working from a Different Location


### 5.1 Temporary Remote Work (Domestic)

Employees may request to work temporarily from a different location within the United States for a period not exceeding **4 weeks** per calendar year. Requests must be approved by your manager and are subject to business needs and tax/legal review.


### 5.2 Temporary Remote Work (International)

Working from outside the United States is generally not permitted due to significant tax, legal, and security complexities. Any request for international remote work must be escalated to the P&C and Legal departments for review and approval. Approval is rare and will only be granted for extenuating circumstances. Unapproved international work is a serious policy violation.


### 5.3 Extended Remote Work (Domestic)

For requests to work from another U.S. state for a period exceeding 4 weeks, the employee must file a request with the **P&C team**. This is required for tax and compliance purposes, as it may create a "nexus" for Innovatech in that state.


- **Procedure:**

    1.  Employee submits a "Change of Work Location" request in WorkDay.

    2.  Manager approves the request.

    3.  P&C and Finance conduct a tax review.

    4.  Final approval is provided by P&C.

- Work from another state that is not reported and approved is a violation of policy and may lead to disciplinary action.


## 6.0 Approval Process

All remote work arrangements, whether formal (hybrid/fully remote) or temporary (from a different location), must be documented and approved by your manager.
