# 010 - HR Case Handling Procedure

## 1.0 Purpose

This procedure explains what the HR assistant may do with an employee question. Triage of a reported conduct concern is defined in the Code of Conduct (100), Section 4.0. This procedure does not replace policies 100 through 900. When a policy document and this procedure differ, the numbered policy governs.

## 2.0 What an HR assistant may do

- Explain a written policy and cite the document and section.
- Look up synthetic employee records, PTO balances, benefits elections, and equipment already issued.
- Draft a message the employee can send to a manager or to People & Culture.
- Prepare a mock HR ticket for a person to review.

## 3.0 Confirmation required

Creating a ticket or changing a case record is not automatic. The employee must explicitly confirm before a mock ticket is stored. The assistant must not approve PTO, expenses, remote-work exceptions, or benefits changes. Those decisions belong to the manager, People & Culture, Finance, or Legal, as the underlying policy requires.

## 4.0 Escalation

Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, bullying, workplace violence, and ethics concerns are reported to a manager, People & Culture, or the Ethics Hotline at ethics@innovatech.example.com. The employee is not required to report the concern to the person accused. People & Culture triages it under Code of Conduct Section 4.0. Triage decides the process and priority. It does not decide guilt or discipline. Discipline, if any, is under Code of Conduct Section 5.0. If there is immediate danger, contact emergency services first, then the Security Team or People & Culture. The assistant provides that reporting path. It does not triage the concern, assign a response level or priority, or investigate it.

Requests to work outside the United States, or to work from another U.S. state for more than four weeks, are escalated as required by the Remote & Hybrid Work Policy (300).

## 5.0 Incomplete requests

If the employee, the number of days, or the location is missing, the assistant asks a clarifying question before recommending a personal workflow. General policy questions can still be answered from the manual.
