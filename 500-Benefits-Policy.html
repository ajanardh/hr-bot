# 500 - Benefits & Insurance Policies


## 1.0 Purpose

Innovatech Solutions is proud to offer a competitive benefits package to support the health and well-being of our employees and their families.


## 2.0 Eligibility

- **Full-Time Employees:** Regularly scheduled to work 30 or more hours per week. Benefits are effective on the 1st of the month following 30 days of employment.

- **Part-Time Employees:** Regularly scheduled to work at least 20 hours per week are eligible for medical, dental, and vision coverage. Benefits are effective on the 1st of the month following 60 days of employment.

- **Temporary/Contract Employees:** Not eligible for company-sponsored benefits.


## 3.0 Health & Welfare Benefits


### 3.1 Medical, Dental, and Vision Insurance

Innovatech offers several PPO and HMO plans through our provider, **WellnessCorp**. The company covers 80% of the employee premium and 50% of dependent premiums.


### 3.2 Health Savings Account (HSA) / Flexible Spending Account (FSA)

- Employees enrolled in a High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) are eligible for an HSA. Innovatech contributes $500 annually to your HSA.

- All employees can enroll in a Healthcare FSA or Dependent Care FSA.


### 3.3 Life and Disability Insurance

- **Life Insurance:** Company-paid life insurance equal to 1x your annual salary.

- **Short-Term & Long-Term Disability:** Company-paid disability insurance to protect your income in case of illness or injury.


## 4.0 Retirement Benefits


### 4.1 401(k) Plan

All employees are eligible to participate in the company's 401(k) plan after 30 days of employment. Innovatech matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of eligible compensation. The match vests immediately.


## 5.0 Other Benefits


### 5.1 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

A confidential counseling and referral service is available to all employees and their household members at no cost. This can help with personal, family, and work-related issues.


### 5.2 Wellness Program

Innovatech offers a wellness stipend of up to **$50 per month** to reimburse eligible wellness expenses such as gym memberships, fitness classes, or mental health apps.


### 5.3 Tuition Reimbursement

After one year of full-time employment, employees are eligible for tuition reimbursement of up to **$5,000 per calendar year** for job-related courses or degree programs, subject to manager approval.


## 6.0 Enrollment

- **New Hires:** You must enroll within 30 days of your eligibility date.

- **Open Enrollment:** An annual open enrollment period is held each November, with changes effective January 1.

- **Qualifying Life Events:** You may make changes to your benefits within 30 days of a qualifying life event (e.g., marriage, birth, loss of other coverage).


For all benefits questions, please contact the P&C team or visit the benefits portal.
