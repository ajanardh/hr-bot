# 800 - IT & Equipment Policy


## 1.0 Purpose

This policy outlines the standards for the issuance, use, and return of company-owned IT equipment.


## 2.0 Scope

This policy applies to all employees who are issued company equipment, including laptops, monitors, and peripherals.


## 3.0 Standard Equipment Issuance

All new full-time employees are issued a standard equipment package on their first day, which includes:

- A company-configured laptop (MacBook Pro or Dell XPS, based on role).

- A power adapter.

- A company-branded backpack or messenger bag.


Additional equipment, such as monitors, keyboards, and mice, may be requested through the IT Helpdesk and are subject to manager approval.


## 4.0 Use and Care

- Employees are responsible for the care and security of all company-issued equipment.

- Equipment must be used in accordance with the **Data Security & Acceptable Use Policy (600)** .

- Do not attempt to repair or modify company equipment yourself. All repairs must be handled by the IT department.


## 5.0 Home Office Stipend

As outlined in the **Expense Policy (400)** , eligible remote and hybrid employees can receive a one-time $500 stipend to purchase home office equipment. The equipment purchased with this stipend is the property of the employee. The company-issued laptop remains company property.


## 6.0 Return of Equipment

Upon termination of employment, all company-issued equipment must be returned to the IT department within **5 business days**. A prepaid shipping box will be provided for remote employees. Failure to return equipment may result in a deduction from the final paycheck, where permitted by law.


## 7.0 Software

- Only approved software may be installed on company devices. A list of approved software is available on the IT Portal.

- Requests for new software must be submitted to the IT Helpdesk and will be reviewed for security and licensing compliance.
