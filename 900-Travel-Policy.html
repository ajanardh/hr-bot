# 900 - Travel & Entertainment Policy


## 1.0 Purpose

This policy provides guidelines for employees who travel on company business or incur expenses for client entertainment. It is intended to ensure reasonable, necessary, and cost-effective spending.


## 2.0 Scope

All employees who travel for work or entertain clients on behalf of Innovatech Solutions.


## 3.0 Booking Travel

- **Travel Agency:** All travel must be booked through our designated travel management company, **CorporateTravel**. This ensures compliance with our policies and provides duty-of-care support.

- **Advance Booking:** Flights and hotels should be booked at least 14 days in advance to secure lower rates.

- **Approval:** All travel must be pre-approved by your manager before booking.


## 4.0 Air Travel

- **Class:** Economy class is required for all flights. Premium economy or business class may be approved by a VP for flights exceeding 8 hours.

- **Frequent Flyer Miles:** Personal frequent flyer miles and hotel points may be accrued.


## 5.0 Ground Transportation

- **Airport Transfers:** Use of ride-sharing services (Uber, Lyft) or airport shuttles is preferred. Rental cars should only be used when more cost-effective or necessary for the destination.

- **Personal Vehicle:** If you use a personal vehicle for business travel, you will be reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate.


## 6.0 Lodging

- Hotel accommodations should be booked at a standard, mid-range hotel. The nightly rate should not exceed $300 (excluding taxes and fees) unless the location's market rate is demonstrably higher. In that case, a reasonable rate is acceptable. Use of vacation rentals (Airbnb, VRBO) is permitted with manager approval if it is more cost-effective than a hotel.


## 7.0 Meals & Incidentals

- Meals are reimbursed on a per diem basis of **$75 per day** for travel within the US. For international travel, the GSA per diem rate for that location will apply.

- Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a client entertainment meal with prior approval.


## 8.0 Client Entertainment

- **Purpose:** Entertainment must have a clear business purpose (e.g., building client relationships, discussing a project).

- **Approval:** Pre-approval from a manager is required.

- **Documentation:** Expense reports for entertainment must include the names and companies of all attendees.


## 9.0 Corporate Credit Card

Employees who travel frequently may be issued a corporate credit card. This card is for business expenses only. Personal charges are strictly prohibited. The card must be reconciled monthly via Concur.
